Set up Mox on this iPhone

Three steps, about a minute. Mox works as an app on your home screen; nothing to download from the App Store.

  1. Copy your pairing code

    It connects Mox on this iPhone to your computer.

    Copy pairing code

  2. Add Mox to your home screen

    Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of Safari (in Chrome, at the top right). Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

  3. Open Mox from your home screen

    Find the new Mox icon, open it, and tap Paste pairing code. From there, Mox walks you through notifications and the Share shortcut.

Pair with your computer

Three steps and you're ready

  1. Turn on notifications

    So you know when your computer sends something, even with Mox closed.

    Turn on notifications

    ✓ Done

  2. Add the Send to Mox shortcut

    It lets you send from Photos and Files with Share. Tap Add Shortcut, then come back here.

    Get the shortcut I've added it

    ✓ Done

  3. Connect the shortcut

    1. Tap Copy setup code.
    2. Open Shortcuts, tap … on Send to Mox, and paste into the empty Text box at the top.
    3. Tap ▶ to run it. It should say Mox is ready.
    Copy setup code It says Mox is ready

    The code holds your Mox password: don't share the shortcut after pasting it.
