# Mox beta test (10 minutes)

Thanks for trying Mox before anyone else. You'll set it up on your computer
and phone, send a few things back and forth, and tell us where you got stuck.
Being stuck is useful: it shows what to fix.

## Before you start (not timed)

- **A computer:** a Mac (macOS 13 or later) or a Windows PC.
- **A phone:** an Android phone (Android 8 or later) or an iPhone.
- **A free [Supabase](https://supabase.com) account** with an organization.
  Creating one takes about five minutes. Do it first so it doesn't count
  against the ten.
- Both devices on the internet. They don't need the same Wi-Fi.

Note the time you start each task. If you're stuck for more than a minute,
write down where, then ask for help.

## Tasks

| # | Task | About | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Install Mox on the computer.** Download it from the [latest release](https://github.com/GiamSoo23/Mox/releases/latest) and open it | 1 min | Mox's setup window is open |
| 2 | **Set it up.** Sign in with Supabase and let Mox create or prepare a project | 4 min | A QR code appears |
| 3 | **Pair your phone.** Scan the QR code with the phone's camera and follow the page | 2 min | The phone says it's paired with your computer |
| 4 | **Computer → phone.** Send a photo: select it and press **⌘⇧M** on a Mac or **Ctrl+Shift+M** on Windows | 1 min | The photo is on the phone |
| 5 | **Phone → computer.** Share a photo from your phone's gallery to Mox | 1 min | It's in the computer's Downloads |
| 6 | **Text.** Copy a sentence on the computer and send it the same way. Then paste it on the phone | 1 min | The sentence pastes on the phone |

Bonus, if you have time: close Mox on the phone, send another photo from
the computer, and see whether it still arrives.

## Tell us how it went (2 minutes)

1. Which computer and phone did you use?
2. How long did each task take? Which ones didn't you finish?
3. Where did you get stuck or hesitate? What did you expect to happen?
4. Was any message or button confusing? Quote it if you can.
5. From 1 to 5, how easy was setup? And sending?
6. Would you use Mox? For what?

Send your answers to the person who invited you, or
[open an issue](https://github.com/GiamSoo23/Mox/issues/new) on GitHub.
Remove anything personal from screenshots first.

## Good to know

- **Your files stay yours.** They go to your own Supabase project, not to
  Mox's author. [Security and privacy](../SECURITY.md) explains how.
- **Unsigned apps.** macOS and Windows warn the first time you open Mox,
  because it isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. The
  [install guide](INSTALL.md) shows how to open it anyway.
- **Afterwards.** To remove Mox, follow [Uninstalling](INSTALL.md#uninstalling).
  You can also delete the Supabase project if you created it just for this.

## For the person running the test

- Watch without helping. Step in only after a minute of being stuck, and
  note what you said.
- Write down the time per task and the exact words of any error.
- Each problem you see becomes a GitHub issue with the label `beta`.
